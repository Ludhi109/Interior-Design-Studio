THE ATELIER

Redefining High-End Spaces Since 2011

At AURA Atelier, we believe that an exceptional interior is the ultimate expression of sophisticated living. Born in the heart of urban design, our studio focuses on creating balance between raw architectural structure and tactile comfort.

Every material, from premium Calacatta marble to textured organic textiles, is meticulously curated to curate light, shadow, and scale. We design for individuals who value craftsmanship, discretion, and timeless aesthetics.

THE JOURNEY

Our Work Methodology

01

Initial Consultation

We listen to your desires, layout parameters, functional needs, and design preferences over coffee or video call to establish alignment.

02

Spatial Concept & Layouts

We generate layout options, mood boards, scale configurations, and hand sketches to define the circulation flow of the environment.

03

3D Renders & Sourcing

We create photorealistic 3D visualization options alongside detailed lists of textiles, stone samples, lighting, and plumbing systems.

04

Construction & Styling

Our designers oversee the physical builds, millwork installers, and paint details, styling the final space down to the art and books.
